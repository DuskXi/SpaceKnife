# Unreleased changes · 未发布的更新

**English** · [简体中文](#简体中文)

What is already done for the next version, **0.3.3**, and not in a release yet. When that version is published, this list becomes its release notes and the file starts over.

### What's new

- **Restart and update.** A new version is downloaded and checked in the background (**Settings › About & updates › Download updates automatically**, on by default). Then one click on **Restart and update** puts it in place and opens SpaceKnife again: the Windows installer runs silently, a portable copy swaps its program files (its `data` folder stays, and the old files come back if something can't be replaced), an AppImage replaces its file. The window says "Updated to SpaceKnife …" once. Disk images and deb / rpm packages still open in the system's installer.
- **Nothing left behind by updates.** Update files go into the `updates` folder in SpaceKnife's data folder instead of your Downloads folder, and each start removes those of the version you run or older.
- **Clean uninstall on Windows.** The uninstaller removes the window's caches, the cache and downloaded updates. Its new **Your data** page asks about the snapshots, settings and logs: kept unless you tick **Also delete the snapshots, settings and logs**. A silent uninstall keeps them, or removes them with `--delete-app-data`.
- **Disks**, a new page (the **Disks** button in the title bar, the **Disks** card at the top of the start page's right column, or the command palette): every disk in the computer, scanned or not, as a dashboard of cards that line up section by section across each row. The summary gives the disks, the capacity and free space, the volumes and the fastest link, a bar of the capacity by storage type and a list of findings (nearly full volumes, links below what the disk supports such as a PCIe 5.0 drive in a 4.0 slot or a USB 3 reader on a USB 2 port, a SATA disk on a narrow add-in controller, a volume that grew a lot since its last snapshot, and what a disk's own health record warns of: a critical warning, media errors, little write endurance left, spare blocks running out, heat). Each disk shows how full it is, its interface and link against its top rate, how much was read and written over its life (from the disk's own record: NVMe SMART / Health log, ATA Device Statistics) and since startup, in units that scale with the size, its temperature, power-on time and SSD life left, and its volumes with what SpaceKnife knows of them: file-class mix and largest folders from an open scan, growth across its snapshots. No administrator rights are needed on Windows and macOS (NVMe disks, through IOKit). On Linux a disk's own lifetime record needs root: **Read with administrator rights…** on a disk card asks once (polkit's password dialog, only on that click) and a read-only helper reads every such disk, NVMe and SATA, including drives in USB enclosures whose bridge passes the commands on.
- **About & updates**, a new settings page (also in the command palette, and linked from **Settings › General**): your version and how it was installed, the last and next update check, the update card when there is one, the versions of the components with **Copy version info**, and a timeline of the last 30 releases with their notes in the interface language.
- **Recycle Bin effect.** What lies in the Recycle Bin (the Trash on macOS and Linux) is shaded and hatched inside a coral dashed outline in all three visualizations, and the bin says how much emptying it frees. The dashes march and a light sweeps across now and then; **Settings › Appearance › Recycle Bin effect** makes it still or turns it off, and **Reduce motion** holds it still. Windows `$Recycle.Bin`, macOS `~/.Trash` and `.Trashes`, and the freedesktop.org Trash on Linux (`~/.local/share/Trash`, `.Trash-<uid>`).
- **Headless mode.** `SpaceKnife --headless` runs SpaceKnife without a window and serves its interface to a browser. `--listen <address>` (for example `--listen 0.0.0.0`) opens it to other devices on the network, after a red warning that you answer with **Y** (`--yes` where there is no terminal, such as a service). The printed link carries a random token, the backend stays on 127.0.0.1, only the page it served may connect, and `--tls-cert` / `--tls-key` serve HTTPS. In the browser the title bar names the computer the files are on, paths on it are typed instead of picked, and the delete basket says that deleting happens there, with the same 5 seconds to undo. It runs on Linux servers without a display. On Windows, write `SpaceKnife` without `.exe`: the new `SpaceKnife.com` next to it makes the terminal wait. See [Headless mode](https://spaceknife.kaltsit.dev/docs/headless/).
- **Two new file classes, and far fewer files in Other.** **Code** (source code, compiler and package-manager output: `py`, `js`, `ts`, `cpp`, `pyc`, `lib`, `pdb`, `whl`…) and **Data** (model weights, datasets, scientific data and databases: `safetensors`, `gguf`, `pt`, `onnx`, `parquet`, `arrow`, `jsonl`, `json`, `fits`, `sqlite`, `db`…) have their own colors, and the other classes know more formats: camera RAW and design files, game video and audio, VM disks and memory files, app packages, crash dumps and event logs, e-books and mail stores, more than 430 extensions in all. On a developer's disk, Other dropped from 60% to 34% of the space and from 78% to 33% of the files. If you edited your file classes, your table stays as it is: **Settings › File classes › Restore default classes** switches to the new one and drops your edits.
- **Resizable details panel.** Drag its left edge to make it 280 to 640 pixels wide (the picture always keeps at least 360) and the picture is laid out again as you drag. Double-click the edge for the default width. The width is remembered.

### Fixes

- **The Classes page no longer wraps.** Names broke in the middle, counts before 「个」 and sizes before their unit. Now the page starts with a bar of the whole selection split in the class colors, then one line per class with aligned columns (files, size, share) and a bar; in a narrow panel the counts shorten. Click a class to filter by it. **Code** and **Data** fold away while they hold less than 2% and less than 8 GB of the selection (**Show all classes**).
- **TypeScript is no longer video.** `.ts` and `.mts` are both TypeScript and MPEG transport-stream (and AVCHD) recordings. SpaceKnife now tells them apart by size, without reading the file: over 16 MB, or over 1 MB and a whole number of 188-byte packets (192 for `.mts`), is Video; anything else is Code. Before, every TypeScript file counted and was colored as video.
- **Deleted space no longer shows as unknown.** What was deleted while a scan was monitored (in Explorer, by emptying the Recycle Bin, by another program) stayed counted as used in the open view, so the freed space grew the unknown-space block while the free-space block kept its old size. The view now follows the volume's free space, read again right after each change.

## 简体中文

下一版 **0.3.3** 已经做完、但还没发布的更改。这一版发布时，这里的内容会成为它的发布说明，然后这个文件清空，重新开始记录。

### 新功能

- **重启并更新**：新版本在后台下载并校验（「设置 › 关于与更新 › 自动下载更新」，默认打开），之后点一下「重启并更新」就换上新版本并重新打开 SpaceKnife：Windows 安装版静默安装；便携版替换程序文件（`data` 文件夹保留，有文件替换不了时放回旧文件）；AppImage 替换文件本身。窗口会提示一次「已更新到 SpaceKnife …」。磁盘映像和 deb / rpm 包仍然交给系统的安装器打开。
- **更新不留残余**：更新文件放进 SpaceKnife 数据文件夹里的 `updates`，不再放进「下载」文件夹；每次启动都会删掉正在运行的版本及更旧版本的更新文件。
- **Windows 上干净卸载**：卸载程序会删除界面缓存、缓存和下载的更新。新的「你的数据」一页询问快照、设置和日志：默认保留，勾上「同时删除快照、设置和日志」才删除。静默卸载默认保留，加 `--delete-app-data` 则删除。
- **「本机磁盘」**页（标题栏的「本机磁盘」按钮、开始页右栏最上面的「本机磁盘」卡片，或命令面板）：以仪表盘的形式列出这台电脑上的每一块盘，扫描过的和没扫描过的；同一行的卡片逐段对齐。汇总给出盘数、容量与可用空间、卷数和最快的链路，一条按存储类型分段的容量条，以及一份发现清单（快满的卷；链路比盘支持的慢，比如 PCIe 5.0 的盘插在 4.0 插槽上、USB 3 读卡器接在 USB 2 接口上；SATA 盘接在通道太窄的扩展卡上；卷比上次快照增长了很多；磁盘自己的健康记录里的严重警告、介质错误、写入寿命所剩无几、备用块不足、温度偏高）。每块盘显示用了多少、接口和链路相对最高速率的情况，整个寿命里和开机以来各读写了多少（前者来自磁盘自己的记录：NVMe SMART / 健康日志、ATA 设备统计日志；数值自动换单位），温度、通电时间和固态硬盘的剩余寿命，以及盘上的卷和 SpaceKnife 对它们知道的东西：打开的扫描里的文件类别构成和最大的文件夹，历次快照之间的增长。Windows 和 macOS（NVMe 盘，经 IOKit）都不需要管理员权限；Linux 上磁盘自己的累计记录要 root：点磁盘卡片上的「用管理员权限读取…」才请求一次授权（polkit 的密码框），由只读助手读出所有这类盘，NVMe 和 SATA 都行，桥接芯片转发命令的 USB 硬盘盒也行。
- **「关于与更新」**设置页（命令面板里也有，「设置 › 常规」里有入口）：你的版本和安装方式、上次与下次检查更新的时间、有新版本时的更新卡片、各组件的版本和「复制版本信息」，以及最近 30 个版本的发布时间线，发布说明按界面语言显示。
- **回收站特效**：回收站（macOS 和 Linux 上的废纸篓 / Trash）里的东西，在三种可视化里都蒙上暗纹、围上珊瑚色虚线，回收站本身标出清空后能释放多少。虚线缓缓流动，偶尔扫过一道光；「设置 › 外观 › 回收站特效」可以改成静态或关掉，「减少动画」时保持静止。支持 Windows 的 `$Recycle.Bin`、macOS 的 `~/.Trash` 和 `.Trashes`，以及 Linux 的 freedesktop.org 回收站（`~/.local/share/Trash`、`.Trash-<uid>`）。
- **无头模式**：`SpaceKnife --headless` 不开窗口运行 SpaceKnife，把界面提供给浏览器。`--listen <地址>`（比如 `--listen 0.0.0.0`）向网络上的其他设备开放，开放前会用红色显示警告，要输入 **Y** 才继续（没有终端时，比如作为服务运行，加 `--yes`）。打印出的链接带有随机令牌，后端仍只监听 127.0.0.1，只有它提供的页面能连接；`--tls-cert` / `--tls-key` 提供 HTTPS。在浏览器里，标题栏写明文件在哪台电脑上，那台电脑的路径要输入而不是选择，删除篮会说明删除发生在那里，撤销的 5 秒照常。没有显示器的 Linux 服务器上也能运行。Windows 上请写 `SpaceKnife`，不加 `.exe`：旁边新增的 `SpaceKnife.com` 会让终端等待。见[无头模式](https://spaceknife.kaltsit.dev/zh/docs/headless/)。
- **新增两个文件类别，「其他」少了一大半**：「代码/开发」（源码，以及编译器和包管理器的产物：`py`、`js`、`ts`、`cpp`、`pyc`、`lib`、`pdb`、`whl`……）和「数据/模型」（模型权重、数据集、科学数据和数据库：`safetensors`、`gguf`、`pt`、`onnx`、`parquet`、`arrow`、`jsonl`、`json`、`fits`、`sqlite`、`db`……）有了自己的颜色；其他类别也认识了更多格式：相机 RAW 和设计文件、游戏里的视频和音频、虚拟机磁盘和内存文件、应用安装包、崩溃转储和事件日志、电子书和邮件存档，共 430 多个扩展名。在一台开发用的电脑上，「其他」从占用空间的 60% 降到 34%，文件数从 78% 降到 33%。改过文件类别的话，你的类别表保持不变；「设置 › 文件类别 › 恢复默认类别」会换成新的内置表，你的改动随之放弃。
- **详情面板可以调宽**：拖动左边缘，宽度在 280–640 像素之间（画面始终至少留 360），画面随拖动重新排布；双击左边缘恢复默认宽度。宽度会被记住。

### 修复

- **「类别统计」不再换行**：以前名称从中间断开，文件数在「个」前、大小在单位前折行。现在页面上方是一条按类别配色的分段条，下面每个类别一行，文件数、大小、占比各列对齐，配一条比例条；面板窄时文件数缩写。单击一行就按这一类过滤。「代码/开发」「数据/模型」占选中项不到 2% 且不到 8 GB 时收起来（「显示全部类别」展开）。
- **TypeScript 不再算作视频**：`.ts`、`.mts` 既是 TypeScript 源码，也是 MPEG 传输流（以及 AVCHD）录像。现在按文件大小区分，不读文件内容：16 MB 以上，或 1 MB 以上且大小正好是整数个 188 字节的包（`.mts` 为 192 字节）的算视频，其余算代码。以前所有 TypeScript 文件都按视频计数和着色。
- **删除的空间不再显示成未知空间**：扫描在监控时被删掉的东西（在资源管理器里删、清空回收站、别的程序删除），在已打开的视图里仍按原来的已用空间计算，释放出来的空间就变成了「未知空间」，可用空间块却还是原来的大小。现在视图跟着卷的可用空间走，每次变化后都会重新读取。
